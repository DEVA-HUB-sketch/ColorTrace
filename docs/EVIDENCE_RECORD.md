# ColorTrace Evidence Record Specification

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the digital evidence record generated by ColorTrace for a presumptive field-test result.

The record is designed to provide a standardized, auditable and cryptographically verifiable representation of a field-test event.

The record does not replace laboratory confirmation.
